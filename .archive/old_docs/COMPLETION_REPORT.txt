ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Error Flow (With LLM Integration):

  Error in System
    ↓
  [ErrorDetectionManager] detects 10 error types
    ↓
  [CoreOrchestrator] routes error
    ↓
  [LLMSystemIntegrator] analyzes with reasoning
    ↓
  [OllamaClient] communicates with Ollama
    ↓
  [Local LLM Model] generates insights (or graceful degradation)
    ↓
  [SelfCorrectionOrchestrator] selects strategy
    ↓
  [Strategy Execution] applies correction
    ↓
  [Explanation] generated in natural language
    ↓
  System Resolves & Learns
